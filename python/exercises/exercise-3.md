### Exercise – 3: Loop Practice

**1. Number Guessing**
Choose a secret number, for example `7`. Keep asking the user to guess until they guess correctly.

**Sample**

```text
Guess the number: 4
Wrong! Try again.
Guess the number: 9
Wrong! Try again.
Guess the number: 7
Correct!
```

---

**2. Print Even Numbers**
Take a number `N`. Print all even numbers from `1` to `N`.

**Input**

```text
10
```

**Output**

```text
2
4
6
8
10
```

---

**3. Skip Multiples of 3**
Take a number `N`. Print numbers from `1` to `N`, but do not print numbers that are divisible by `3`.

**Input**

```text
10
```

**Output**

```text
1
2
4
5
7
8
10
```

---

**4. Keep Asking for a Positive Number**
Ask the user to enter a positive number. Keep asking until they enter a number greater than `0`.

**Sample**

```text
Enter a positive number: -5
Invalid! Try again.
Enter a positive number: 0
Invalid! Try again.
Enter a positive number: 8
Thank you!
```

---

**5. Print Numbers Backwards**
Take a number `N` and print numbers from `N` down to `1`.

**Input**

```text
6
```

**Output**

```text
6
5
4
3
2
1
```

---

**6. Stop at Zero**
Keep asking the user to enter numbers. Stop when they enter `0`.

**Sample**

```text
Enter a number: 5
Enter a number: 8
Enter a number: 3
Enter a number: 0
Program ended.
```

---

**7. Skip the Number 7**
Take a number `N`. Print numbers from `1` to `N`, but skip `7`.

**Input**

```text
10
```

**Output**

```text
1
2
3
4
5
6
8
9
10
```

---

**8. Password with Limited Attempts**
Ask the user to enter the password. Give them a maximum of **3 attempts**. The correct password is `"python"`.

**Sample**

```text
Enter password: java
Wrong password.
Enter password: hello
Wrong password.
Enter password: python
Welcome!
```

If they fail all three attempts:

```text
Too many attempts. Access denied.
```

---

**9. Sum Until Zero**
Keep asking the user to enter numbers. Add all the numbers together. Stop when the user enters `0`, then print the total.

**Sample**

```text
Enter number: 5
Enter number: 3
Enter number: 7
Enter number: 0
Total = 15
```

---

**10. Print Multiplication Table**
Take a number and print its multiplication table from `1` to `10`.

**Input**

```text
5
```

**Output**

```text
5
10
15
20
25
30
35
40
45
50
```

### Exercise – 4: A Little More Practice

**11. Count from 1 to N**

**12. Count Even Numbers**
Take a number `N` and count how many even numbers are between `1` and `N`.

**Input**

```text
10
```

**Output**

```text
There are 5 even numbers.
```

**13. Find the Sum**
Take a number `N` and find the sum of numbers from `1` to `N`.

**Input**

```text
5
```

**Output**

```text
Sum = 15
```

**14. Print Odd Numbers**
Take a number `N` and print all odd numbers from `1` to `N`.

**15. Repeat Until "stop"**
Keep asking the user to enter a word. Stop when they enter `"stop"`.

**Sample**

```text
Enter a word: hello
Enter a word: python
Enter a word: loop
Enter a word: stop
Goodbye!
```

**16. Find the Largest Number**
Ask the user to enter **5 numbers**. After all five numbers are entered, print the largest number.

**17. Count Positive and Negative Numbers**
Ask the user to enter **5 numbers**. Count how many are positive and how many are negative.

**18. Print a Star Pattern**
Take a number `N` and print:

**Input**

```text
5
```

**Output**

```text
*
**
***
****
*****
```
