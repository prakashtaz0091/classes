# Ratio Word Problems – Practice Exercise
1. Boys and Girls: In a classroom, the ratio of boys to girls is 3:2. If there are 18 boys, how many girls are there?

2. Apples and Oranges: A fruit basket contains apples and oranges in the ratio 5:3. If there are 40 fruits altogether, how many apples and oranges are there?

3. Sharing Money: Ram and Shyam share Rs. 600 in the ratio 2:3. How much money does each person receive?

4. Red and Blue Balls: A bag contains red and blue balls in the ratio 4:7. If there are 28 blue balls, how many red balls are there?

5. Students in a School: The ratio of teachers to students in a school is 1:20. If there are 15 teachers, how many students are there?
   
6. Sharing Chocolates: Three friends, A, B, and C, share 120 chocolates in the ratio 2:3:5. How many chocolates does each friend receive?

7. Mixing Juice: A juice seller mixes orange juice and water in the ratio 5:2. If he wants to prepare 35 liters of the mixture, how many liters of orange juice and water are needed?

8. Length and Width: The length and width of a rectangular garden are in the ratio 7:4. If the perimeter of the garden is 88 meters, find its length and width.

9. Money Distribution: A father divides Rs. 9,600 between his two children in the ratio 5:7. How much more money does the second child receive than the first?
